Spend & Contract Details

Your Azure spend profile and contract structure determine your baseline negotiating position.

Anniversary true-ups are when seats and products can be added at your EA's locked pricing.

The July 1, 2026 Microsoft 365 increase applies at the next renewal after that date. A term that began earlier keeps pre-increase pricing until renewal, so renewing early brings the increase forward rather than avoiding it.
