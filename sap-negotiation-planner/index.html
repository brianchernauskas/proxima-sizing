SAP Estate & Timing

SAP negotiations turn on one question: where is the estate in its move from ECC and annual maintenance to S/4HANA and subscription cloud? SAP rewards cloud backlog, ECC mainstream maintenance ends December 31, 2027, and SAP's fiscal year follows the calendar, so Q4 and year end matter most.

Footprint, Migration & Compliance

Leverage, Context & M&A

SAP prices against the cost of leaving, and the cost of leaving ERP is high. What changes the conversation is a credible third-party support quote, a self-hosted S/4HANA option costed against RISE, a hyperscaler commitment that can absorb the hosting, or a corporate transaction that changes which entities use SAP.
