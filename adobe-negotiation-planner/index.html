This tool provides strategic guidance based on publicly reported Adobe contracting practices and procurement benchmarks. Adobe does not publish ETLA discount or renewal increase data — all ranges are directional estimates, not vendor-confirmed figures. Actual outcomes depend on agreement type, reseller involvement, timing within Adobe's fiscal year, and your credible alternatives. Nothing here is legal advice on M&A contract assignment — have counsel read the actual clauses.
