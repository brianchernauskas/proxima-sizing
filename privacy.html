Privacy Statement

Last updated October 1, 2026

This statement covers the Proxima tools hub and the Proxima planner and reference tools linked from it, hosted on GitHub Pages. The tools are internal and intended for Proxima use. They are built to keep the information you work with on your own device.

Information you enter into the tools

The planners and diagnostic tools run in your browser. Spend figures, contract terms, uploaded files and other inputs are processed locally on your device and are not sent to Proxima or to any server we operate. Exports, such as PDFs or anonymised files, are generated locally and saved only where you choose to save them.

Please still avoid entering information you are not permitted to share, and review any export before passing it on.

What we collect

  • Usage analytics. The pages use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which tools are used. It collects standard page-view data such as pages visited, approximate location, browser and device type, and uses cookies or similar identifiers to do so. It does not receive anything you type into a tool.
  • Hosting logs. The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as IP address and request details under its own privacy statement.

What is stored in your browser

These tools do not require a password or sign-in. Some tools may keep preferences, such as a theme choice, in your browser's local storage; you can remove them at any time by clearing the site data for this domain.

Access gate

The tools are intended for Proxima use but are publicly reachable and have no access control. Do not enter confidential client data into them or place it on this site.

Third parties and sharing

We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising. Analytics data is processed by Google, and the site is hosted by GitHub. The hub's news and digest pages fetch their content from this same site.

Your choices

  • Block analytics with your browser's tracking protection, a content blocker, or the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. The tools work without it.
  • Clear cookies and site data for this domain at any time.

Changes and questions

This statement may be updated as the tools change; the date above shows the latest revision. Questions or requests: contact Brian Chernauskas.
